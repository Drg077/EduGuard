Risk Score Interpretation Framework:

The dropout-risk score is calculated as the model's posterior probability of the Dropout outcome class: \( P(\text{Dropout} \mid X) \in [0.0, 1.0] \). This value represents the estimated risk based on the student's recorded curricular performance and demographics. It is an indicative early-warning score to prioritize advisor attention, not an absolute certainty of student dropout.
